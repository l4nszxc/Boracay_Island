About Boracay & Our Agency

Paradise Reimagined: Sustainable, World-Class Tourism

Boracay is a butterfly-shaped island located in the Western Visayas region of the Philippines, approximately 315 kilometers south of Manila. Celebrated globally for its 4-kilometer stretch of powdery white sand along White Beach, tranquil turquoise waters, and sensational sunsets, the island represents the crowning jewel of Philippine tropical tourism.

Our Mission

To deliver authentic, seamless, and eco-friendly island experiences that respect local culture and support community livelihoods.

Our Vision

To be the Philippines' premier digital tourism portal inspiring sustainable adventures and unforgettable lifelong memories.

  • Tourism Significance: Major driver of national revenue and cultural pride with modern environmental preservation policies.
  • Distinct Character: Divided into 3 vibrant beach stations and pristine northern coves offering distinct leisure vibes.
  • Target Market: International globe-trotters, Filipino holidaymakers, families, thrill-seekers, and honeymooners.

How to Get There

Getting to Boracay Island

Reaching Boracay is streamlined via direct flights to Caticlan Airport followed by a scenic 10-minute jetty boat crossing to Cagban Port.

Direct 45-minute commercial flights operate daily from Manila (MNL) and Cebu (CEB) to Godofredo P. Ramos Airport (Caticlan). From Caticlan Airport, it is just a 5-minute electric tricycle (e-trike) ride to Caticlan Jetty Port.

Present your DOT-accredited hotel booking confirmation voucher at the terminal. Board the traditional motorized banca or modern air-conditioned fastcraft for a breezy 10-to-15 minute ride across the tablas strait directly to Cagban Port in Boracay.

From Cagban Port, clean green e-trikes or private package hotel vans transfer you to your hotel along Station 1, 2, or 3 within 15–25 minutes.

Essential Island Knowledge

Tourist Travel Guide & Guidelines

Ensure a smooth, safe, and environmentally sustainable visit by reviewing our comprehensive travel reminders.

Best Time & Weather
Amihan vs Habagat
  • Amihan Season (Nov - May): Peak season! Calm, glassy seas on White Beach with sunny skies.
  • Habagat Season (Jun - Oct): Southwest monsoon. Windy on White Beach, watersports shift to Bulabog. Great budget travel rates.
What to Bring & Dress
Packing Essentials
  • Reef-safe biodegradable sunscreen (SPF 50+) to safeguard coral formations.
  • Waterproof phone pouch & dry bag for island hopping boat excursions.
  • Breathable linen, swimwear, UV rash guards, sunglasses, and flip-flops.
Eco Rules & Etiquette
Strict Island Preservation
  • No Eating or Drinking on White Beach: Dining is restricted to restaurant premises to avoid trash.
  • No Smoking & Liquor on Sand: Strictly enforced by Malay tourism police with fines.
  • Never collect or take sand, pebbles, or shells from the beach.
Money & Payments
Currency Considerations
  • Currency: Philippine Peso (PHP, ₱). ATMs are available in D'Mall, Station 1 & 2.
  • Major credit cards & GCash / Maya digital wallets accepted at resorts and restaurants.
  • Keep small cash bills (₱20, ₱50, ₱100) handy for e-trikes and beach vendors.
Getting Around Boracay
Eco Electric Tricycles
  • E-trikes operate on the main island road: standard fare is ₱15 - ₱25 per pax along stations.
  • Special direct trip (charter) across stations ranges from ₱100 - ₱150 per e-trike.
  • The 4km White Beach beachfront is 100% pedestrian-only and ideal for strolling.
Health & Emergency
24/7 Hotlines
  • Boracay Tourist Police: (036) 288-3066 / 166
